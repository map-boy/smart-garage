C & V SMART GARAGE - Reception & Stock
=======================================

Install this on the reception desk PC and/or the stock room PC.

1. Run the .exe. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" -
   click "More info" then "Run anyway" (the installer is unsigned,
   this is expected).
2. Follow the installer.
3. Open "C & V Smart Garage" from the Start menu.
4. Login password: smartgarage

This app works offline and syncs to the cloud automatically when
there is internet. New clients and stock changes made here appear
on the Admin/Boss app on their own, usually within seconds of
coming back online.
